We believe you deserve to know exactly what's behind the "Analyze" button. Here are the real numbers — strengths, limits, and all.

🔧 The pipeline

  1. Cleaning: lowercase, strip URLs/punctuation.
  2. TF-IDF vectorizer (1–2 grams, 5,000 features) turns words into numbers.
  3. Logistic Regression (multinomial, balanced classes) predicts low / moderate / high.
  4. Group-aware split: paraphrases of the same example never leak across train/test — the metrics below are honest.
